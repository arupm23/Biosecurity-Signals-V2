Method & scope

Trace claims back to evidence

Biosecurity Signals organizes statements from model cards, research papers, developer documentation, and institutional guidance. These are public claims and documented evaluations, not independent safety certifications.

  1. 01
    Identify a primary source

    Preserve the source URL, title, publication period and evidence type for every record.

  2. 02
    Classify each safeguard

    Link individual reported practices to one of four lifecycle layers and their supporting source.

  3. 03
    Preserve limitations and review history

    Keep explicit caveats, distinguish independent evaluation from developer disclosure, and document changes.

Interpretation matters: “Not reported” does not mean “not implemented.” An independent evaluation being documented is not proof that a tool is safe. Guidance and case studies describe recommendations or findings, not necessarily operational safeguards. This repository contains no biological sequences, protocols, or circumvention instructions.
