# Attribution and intellectual lineage

Biosecurity Signals is an independent implementation created by **Arup Mohanty**.

## Foundational project idea

- Max Langenkamp and Tessa Alexanian, “Five Tractable Biosecurity Projects You Could Start Tomorrow,” EA Forum, 31 January 2025. https://forum.effectivealtruism.org/posts/RwMpnHwqhTZ2rrwbr/five-tractable-biosecurity-projects-you-could-start-tomorrow
- Shiying He and P.T. Nhean, “Biosecurity Statements Repository,” EA Forum, 3 October 2025. https://forum.effectivealtruism.org/posts/7ZAgSrpEZKScNfXtD/biosecurity-statements-repository

Their spreadsheet-based repository and category framework helped establish the approach. Biosecurity Signals does not claim authorship of those original ideas or their spreadsheet. The web interface, curated sample data, source-linked evidence profiles and interactive views are independently implemented.
